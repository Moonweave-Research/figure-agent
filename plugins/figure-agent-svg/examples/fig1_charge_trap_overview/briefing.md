# Fig. 1 Charge-Trap Electret Actuator Overview

## Topic

Three-panel scientific overview for a charge-trap electret actuator manuscript.

## Composition

- Panel A: layered elastomer/electret/electrode stack with charge injection.
- Panel B: qualitative trap-depth landscape with shallow and deep traps.
- Panel C: long-retention curve coupled to electrostatic bending actuation.

## Invariants

The figure is schematic, not a measured data plot. Trap depth is qualitative;
deep traps must sit lower than shallow traps. The actuator panel should imply
retained charge driving bending without encoding measured values.
