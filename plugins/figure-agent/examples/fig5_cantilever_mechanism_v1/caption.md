# Caption — fig5_cantilever_mechanism_v1

**Figure 5 | Charge-to-actuation pathway.** A sulfur-polymer specimen is first
charged by a two-terminal high-voltage path without a charging-stage ground or
grid. The charged specimen is then manually transferred to the measurement
station, where the conductive substrate is grounded and an induction-type
electrostatic surface voltmeter (ESVM) reads the non-contact surface potential.
The final panel isolates the mechanical consequence: a vertically hanging,
electrically floating polymer cantilever bends away from a nearby driven
electrode across an air gap under the Coulomb force of trapped charge. All
geometries are qualitative and schematic; no voltage, force, displacement, or
time scale is implied.
