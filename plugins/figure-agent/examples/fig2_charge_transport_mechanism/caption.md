# Caption — fig2_charge_transport_mechanism

**Fig. 2a | Charge-transport mechanism schematic.** Measurement context for a
field-on MIM charging-current experiment, followed by a conceptual conventional
dielectric reference and a sulfur-rich copolymer with sign-neutral localized-state
cues. The applied field is held on throughout acquisition. The final icon shows
the late current relative to each material's own early power-law reference:
sulfur compositions at 70 wt% and above rise above that reference, whereas
PI/PTFE controls fall below it. The strip is schematic; quantitative currents,
exponents, controls, and late-time deviation are supplied by the data pipeline in
panels b–d. The figure does not assign carrier polarity, trap density, trap depth,
leakage suppression, or a microscopic transport pathway.
