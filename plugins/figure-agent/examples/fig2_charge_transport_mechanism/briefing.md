# Briefing — fig2_charge_transport_mechanism

> **Genre**: Nature Communications main-text Fig. 2a, full-width mechanism
> schematic above three quantitative data panels. The data panels are owned by
> the charge-transport data pipeline; Figure Agent draws this reader-facing
> schematic only.

## §1 Story slot

Fig. 1 establishes the sulfur-rich copolymer platform and the measurement axes.
Fig. 2 is the first data-substance figure: it tests whether sulfur-rich
copolymers retain charge differently from conventional dielectrics and whether
the late-time response departs from the early power law. Fig. 3 owns the
frequency-domain dielectric response; Fig. 4 owns ISPD trap-distribution
inference; Fig. 5 owns the floating-clip actuation mechanism.

The strip must read left to right:

`MIM measurement context → conventional finite-trap drain → sulfur-rich broad
trap landscape with retained charge → the two current-decay windows read in
panels b–d`.

## §2 Panel content

- **Measurement**: a compact MIM stack with the film between electrodes, the
  declared field condition `$E=15\,\mathrm{MV\,m^{-1}}$`, and `25 %RH` context.
- **Conventional dielectric**: a finite/shallow trap population with visibly
  outward drain cues. This is a conceptual contrast, not a universal law.
- **Sulfur-rich copolymer**: a distributed, non-periodic trap population inside
  the film and a retained-charge cue. Do not redraw Fig. 1's energy diagram.
- **What we read**: one qualitative decay trace split into a solid `2–30 s`
  window and a dashed `30–300 s` window. It is a readout icon, not data.

## §3 Physics invariants

- The MIM stack and field condition are measurement context, not a charging
  instrument or a second actuation setup.
- The conventional-versus-sulfur comparison is a working schematic that
  motivates the measured transient contrast; it is not a new proof of a
  microscopic mechanism.
- The two time windows are independent readout windows: early power-law slope
  and late-time deviation are related but not the same metric.
- Quantitative `I(t)`, exponent, control-band, and normalized-deviation curves
  belong to the data pipeline and must not be approximated by this strip.

## §4 Must avoid

- No exact exponent, resistivity, sample code, error bar, or axis tick.
- No S–S homolytic-cleavage energy diagram; that meaning is already assigned to
  Fig. 1 / Fig. 4 and would duplicate the manuscript story.
- No claim that the strip alone proves the broad-trap microscopic origin.
- No oversized dashed UI-style card or text-only placeholder; the objects must
  carry the comparison.
